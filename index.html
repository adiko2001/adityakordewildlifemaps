Fieldwork and Spatial Data

I am an early-career wildlife conservationist focusing on large mammal ecology, linear disturbance mitigation, and practical spatial data cleaning across Western Canada.

My work combines physically deploying and servicing remote camera arrays with spatial modeling in ArcGIS Pro and QGIS, converting provincial forest tenure and wildfire databases into actionable management plans.
